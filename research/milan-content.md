## 3. Sights & experiences — 14

1. **Duomo di Milano + rooftop terraces** — **Google 4.8 (110,035)** ([seethebigworld](https://seethebigworld.com/en/duomo-di-milano-review/)) · 45.4642, 9.1900. Open daily ~9:00–19:00 (last entry 18:10). 2026 prices: cathedral €10; terraces-by-stairs from €16; **combo cathedral+terraces €26 adult / €13 child 6–18**; lift costs more ([alongdustyroads](https://www.alongdustyroads.com/posts/duomo-milan-cathedral-tickets), [GYG](https://www.getyourguide.com/milan-l139/duomo-and-terraces-ticket-with-audioguide-t153354/)). Book a timed slot online a few days ahead for July; go at 09:00 before the heat. **Dress code enforced: shoulders + knees covered** (adults and kids). Time: 1.5–2 h. The rooftop walk among marble spires is the single best "wow" for kids.
2. **Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II** — Google rating **not verifiable in this research**; TA 4.5 ([TA](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g187849-d194342-Reviews-Galleria_Vittorio_Emanuele_II-Milan_Lombardy.html)) · 45.4659, 9.1900. Free, always open. Do the **bull-mosaic spin**: heel on the Turin bull, three turns for luck (mosaic restored again in 2026 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galleria_Vittorio_Emanuele_II)). 20–30 min.
3. **Castello Sforzesco** — 4.5 (13,806 reviews, TA-sourced snippet; Google not directly verified) ([TA](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g187849-d246672-Reviews-Castello_Sforzesco-Milan_Lombardy.html)) · 45.4704, 9.1793. Courtyards **free**, daily; the 7 museums (incl. Michelangelo's Pietà Rondanini) one ticket **€5, under-18 free, museums closed Mon** ([introducingmilan](https://www.introducingmilan.com/castello-sforzesco)). 1–1.5 h with kids.

## 4. Suggested itinerary

### Thursday 9 July — land MXP 22:55 (late-night transfer)
- **Malpensa Express (recommended):** trains run from **T1 until 00:26** — after the 22:55 landing + bags (~23:30–23:45 realistic), target the **23:56 or 00:26 departure** (≈37–43 min to town; evening trains run to Cadorna at :27/:57 pattern from the city side, last city departure 23:27, last airport departure 00:26) ([malpensaexpress.it timetable](https://www.malpensaexpress.it/en/lines-and-timetable/lines-and-timetables/timetable/), [trenord.it](https://www.trenord.it/en/routes-and-timetables/services/airport-routes-mxp/)). ~€13 pp one-way (~); kids under 14 free with the Trenord family concession (Section 6). There is also a **night bus from T1 at 01:30 → Cadorna** as a safety net ([malpensaexpress.it news](https://www.malpensaexpress.it/en/news/trenord-information/news/new-night-run-from-malpensa-to-milan/)).
- **Fixed-fare taxi:** official flat fare MXP↔Milan in 2026 is **~€100–115 depending on source/destination** — SEA airport tariff €110 (Feb 2026), Comune flat €100, Centrale-specific quotes ~€114; the old "€115" figure is the upper end. ~50 min at midnight. Confirm the flat fare with the driver before boarding ([milanobycar 2026 guide](https://www.milanobycar.com/blog/how-much-is-a-taxi-from-milan-to-malpensa), [taxiblu](https://taxiblu.it/en/fares-taxi-milan/)).
- **Recommendation:** with two sleepy kids, take the train **if** you're staying near Centrale and bags arrive fast; otherwise the fixed-fare taxi door-to-door (~€110 ≈ €27.50 pp) is worth it at midnight. Pre-book nothing; both options are turn-up.

### Friday 10 July — classic Milan, kid-paced
- 08:30 breakfast near hotel → **09:00 Duomo + rooftop** (pre-booked slot, dress code) → bull-spin in the **Galleria** → 11:30 **panzerotto from Luini** (it's Friday — open) + **Artico gelato (Via Dogana)**.
- Ride **historic tram 1** to **Castello Sforzesco** (courtyards + one museum, €5 adults/kids free) → picnic + run-around in **Parco Sempione**.
- 15:00 option A: **Science Museum + Toti submarine** (pre-book Toti) — 10 min walk from the park; option B (if Last Supper tickets were scored for ~16:00): **Cenacolo**, then the Science Museum moves to Saturday-in-Milan plan.
- Evening: metro/walk to **Brera** lanes for golden hour → **treat dinner at Ratanà** (book) with **Bosco Verticale/Gae Aulenti** stroll after, or pizza at **Pizza AM**. Gelato stop #2: Gelateria della Musica if you're south, Artico Isola if north.

### Saturday 11 July — **Lake Como (recommended)** vs second Milan day
**Case for Como (recommended — the family loves water):**
- Train **Milano Centrale → Varenna-Esino ~1h05, roughly hourly including weekends** (Tirano/Sondrio line; ~18 trains/day; from ~€7.30 adult, kids <14 free with family concession) ([trenord.it](https://www.trenord.it/en/routes-and-timetables/most-searched-lines/milano-central-station-varenna-esino/), [trainline](https://www.thetrainline.com/en/train-times/milan-central-station-to-varenna-esino)). Take the ~08:20 train; buy seats/check times for the exact Saturday — July trains get full, arrive 25 min early.
- **Mid-lake ferry triangle:** Varenna–Bellagio–Menaggio crossings every **30–60 min, ~15 min per hop, 7:00–20:00ish in summer**; single €5.50, **mid-lake day pass €15.20** (unlimited Varenna/Bellagio/Menaggio/Cadenabbia) ([navigazionelaghi.it](https://www.navigazionelaghi.it/en/tickets-and-timetables-lake-como/), [lakecomotravel](https://lakecomotravel.com/boat-tours-ferry-lake-como/)).
- **Swimming:** **Lido di Menaggio** — sandy beach + adult & kids' pools, playground, restaurant (best family pick, ~€35–50 for sunbeds+umbrella, ~) ([explorelakecomo](https://www.explorelakecomo.com/cat/lido-clubs), [bookingsforyou](https://bookingsforyou.com/inspiration/lake-como-holiday-guide/lake-como-beaches/)); or **Lido di Bellagio** (beach club between town and Villa Melzi, open ~10:30–18:30, ~€50 for 2 with beds, ~) ([lidodibellagio.com](https://www.lidodibellagio.com/beach/)); free pebble option: Varenna's Riva Grande.
- Flow: Varenna passerella walk → ferry to Bellagio (gelato + steep lanes) → ferry to Menaggio for lido swim afternoon → ferry back to Varenna → train home ~18:30–19:30 (last trains ~21:20). Pack swim gear from Dublin.
**Case for Milan instead:** zero logistics, San Siro tour (family pack €112) + Science Museum/Toti + Navigami canal cruise + rooftop-pool downtime; better if Friday over-ran or a heatwave/thunderstorms hit.
**Verdict: Como.** It's the water day this family wants, it's genuinely 65 min door-to-door, kids ride trains free, and mid-July ferries run constantly. Keep San Siro/Navigami as the rain plan B.

### Sunday 12 July — morning + BGY for 17:25
Two routes to BGY (~50 km):
- **Direct airport coach from Milano Centrale:** Terravision / Autostradale / FlixBus / Flibco, ~every 20–30 min, **50–60 min, ~€5–10** ([terravision](https://www.terravision.eu/airport_transfer/bus-bergamo-airport-milan/), [milanbergamoairport.it/bus](https://www.milanbergamoairport.it/en/bus/)).
- **Train Milano Centrale → Bergamo: direct, ~48 min, from €6.00, roughly hourly** ([trenord.it](https://www.trenord.it/en/routes-and-timetables/most-searched-lines/milano-centrale-bergamo-route/)), then **ATB bus Line 1** station→airport **10–15 min, €2.60** (90-min ticket also covers the funicular) ([tourismattractions BGY guide](https://tourismattractions.net/italy/bergamo-airport-shuttle-info)).

**Plan A — Città Alta stop (verdict: YES, comfortably doable):**
- ~08:45 train Centrale→Bergamo (arr ~09:35). **Left luggage at Bergamo station exit: open daily 7:00–20:00** (attendant; backup tel. 366 5940993) ([visitbergamo.net](https://www.visitbergamo.net/en/luggage-storage/)); app alternatives (Radical/Bounce/LuggageHero) from ~€2–5/bag/day nearby. BGY airport also has a **left-luggage office** ([milanbergamoairport.it](https://www.milanbergamoairport.it/en/left-luggage-office/) — hours/price not verifiable in this research, so use the station).
- Bus 1 or walk to the **funicular** (covered by the €2.60 ATB ticket; **children under 10 free**) → **Piazza Vecchia**, Venetian walls, polenta-and-gelato "polenta e osei" sweets; early lunch ~12:15.
- ~13:30 funicular down, collect bags ~13:50, **ATB Line 1 to BGY ~14:10–14:30** → airport ~15:00, 2h25 before the 17:25 flight. Buffer everywhere; if anything slips, taxis station→BGY are ~15 min.
- Note Sunday closures (Section 6): Luini shut, so do any last Milan food shopping Saturday.
**Plan B — straight to BGY:** easy Milan morning (Sempione playgrounds / Darsena stroll / last gelato), 13:00 coach from Centrale, airport by ~14:10. Choose B if it's stormy or anyone's flagging.

## 5. Neighbourhood guide

- **Duomo/Centro storico** — Marble, mosaics, crowds; everything walkable (Duomo→Castello 15 min). Where first-timers stay if budget allows (Giulia, Spadari, Ostello Bello Duomo); eat at Nerino Dieci, Luini, Piz. Pricey, touristy at lunch.
- **Brera** — Cobbled artists' quarter just north; galleries, boutiques, the Pinacoteca. Prettiest evening stroll in Milan; eat at La Libera (Google 4.3, [RG](https://restaurantguru.com/La-Libera-Milan)). Boutique stays, few family rooms.
- **Navigli/Ticinese** — Canal-side, young, buzzing from 18:00 (aperitivo); street food, gelato, vintage shops. 21HoS Navigli (pool) here; Conchetta and Gelateria della Musica nearby. Lively at night — fine for families before ~22:00.
- **Porta Nuova/Isola** — Glass skyscrapers + Bosco Verticale next to an old village grid; modern, safe, great playgrounds (BAM park). Ratanà, Artico Isola. Aparthotels and design hotels.
- **Centrale/Buenos Aires** — Transport king (MXP train, BGY coaches, metro M2/M3, Como/Bergamo trains). Blocks around the station are functional rather than charming — keep wits about you at night, it's fine. Berna, Ostello Bello Centrale, 43 Station; Pavé and Miscusi nearby. Corso Buenos Aires = saldi shopping.
- **Sempione/Magenta** — Elegant residential west: Castello, Parco Sempione, Santa Maria delle Grazie + Science Museum, Pizzium Procaccini. Quiet, leafy, very pram/kid friendly.

## 6. Practical facts

- **Weather mid-July:** ~29–31 °C highs / ~19–20 °C nights, humid (Po Valley), sun + occasional late-day thunderstorms (~5 rain days/67 mm in July) ([weather-atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/italy/milan-weather-july), [climatestotravel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/italy/milan)). Hats, refillable bottles, do outdoor sights before noon.
- **ATM transport (2026):** single **€2.20** (90 min, metro+tram+bus); 24h €7.60; 3-day €15.50. Paper tickets phased out — **tap contactless cards/phones at gates; daily cap €7.60**. **Children under 14 travel FREE** with a paying adult (max 2 kids/adult; carry ID showing age) ([introducingmilan](https://www.introducingmilan.com/tickets-and-travel-cards), [yesmilano](https://www.yesmilano.it/en/articles/traveling-with-children-public-transport), [mediolan.pl](https://mediolan.pl/en/tickets-for-the-metro-and-public-transport-in-milan/)). So the whole family rides for 2 adult taps.
- **Trenord trains (Como/Bergamo/MXP):** "**Io viaggio in famiglia**" — under-14s ride **free** on Lombardy regional trains with an adult relative holding a valid ticket; print the free form/PDF before travel ([trenord.it](https://www.trenord.it/en/tickets/concessions-and-extras/travelling-with-your-family/)).
- **Saldi:** summer sales in Lombardy start **Saturday 4 July 2026** (until 1 Sep) — the trip lands in week one: Corso Buenos Aires + Via Torino for real bargains ([milanopocket](https://www.milanopocket.it/saldi-estivi-2026-lombardia/), [Regione Lombardia](https://www.regione.lombardia.it/attivita-produttive-imprese/commercio/commercio-al-dettaglio-in-sede-fissa/date-e-regole-dei-saldi)).
- **Duomo dress code:** shoulders and knees covered, all ages, enforced at security; no sleeveless tops/short shorts ([smilingway](https://smilingway.cz/en/duomo-di-milano/)). Carry a light scarf in the daypack.
- **Pickpockets:** classic hotspots are **Centrale station concourse, the Duomo square/metro, and busy trams**; usual tactics = "help" at ticket machines, friendship bracelets, distraction bumps. Phones in front pockets, bags zipped and forward. Petty theft, not violence.
- **Vedovelle:** ~400–600 green cast-iron "little widow"/green-dragon fountains dispense free, safe, continuously tested drinking water — refill bottles all day ([yesmilano](https://www.yesmilano.it/en/articles/useful-information-your-visit), [memorialdrinkingfountains](https://memorialdrinkingfountains.wordpress.com/2019/03/30/vedovelle-fountains/)).
- **Closures affecting THIS trip:** Monday closures (Last Supper, Brera, castle museums, Science Museum) don't bite — no Monday in trip. **Sunday**: Luini closed; Piz closed; many small shops shut — but it's departure morning anyway. Friday/Saturday everything is open.
- **Tipping:** not expected. Most restaurants add **coperto** (€1–3 pp) and that's that; round up or leave €2–5 for great service. Never a percentage obligation.
- **EU roaming (Irish SIMs):** "Roam like at Home" — calls/texts/data in Italy at Irish domestic rates through 2026; only watch provider fair-use data caps (~20–30 GB on unlimited plans) ([ComReg](https://www.comreg.ie/advice-information/roaming-2/using-your-mobile-within-the-eu/), [europa.eu](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/internet-telecoms/mobile-roaming-costs/index_en.htm)). No eSIM needed.

**Milan's ring structure:** the city grew in concentric rings around the Duomo — the innermost, the **Cerchia dei Navigli**, traces the medieval canal moat (filled in during the 1930s; today's inner ring road of Via Senato–Via F. Sforza–Via Molino delle Armi follows it). The surviving water is at the **Darsena**, the old city port, where the **Naviglio Grande** (built from the 12th c. to float marble in from Lake Maggiore for the Duomo) arrives from the west and the **Naviglio Pavese** sets off south toward Pavia — the two canals that give the Navigli district its name and its evening life.
