## 1. Restaurants

**Honest headline:** Rabat's famous "tourist icon" restaurants (Dinarjat, Le Dhow, Dar Naji, La Mamma) mostly sit *below* the 4.5-star bar on Google — they're included below but clearly flagged. The genuinely top-rated (4.7–4.9) places are newer bistros in Agdal/Hay Riad. A good mix for the family is: medina riad-restaurants for atmosphere, Agdal/Hay Riad for food quality, and the Kasbah/beach for views.

### Top-rated (verified ≥4.5)

**1. HUNA | Eat & Drink** — Mediterranean all-day restaurant & brunch
- **Google 4.8, ~3,000 reviews** — the most-reviewed restaurant in Rabat (per Heerby's 2026 round-up: https://heerby.ma/en/blog/best-restaurants-rabat). Note TA is much lower (4.0, ranked ~#202 — tourists vs locals split: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d27885028-Reviews-Huna_Eat_Drink-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html).
- Cuisine: modern Mediterranean sharing plates, à la carte brunch, homemade desserts (https://huna.ma/en/home/).
- Area: Hay Riad (Mahaj Ryad shopping/dining district, south Rabat). Price: €€. Family-friendly: yes (all-day menu, casual).
- Lat/long: ~33.9590, -6.8660 (Mahaj Ryad, approx.)
- Why go: the city's go-to stylish all-day spot — safe bet for a relaxed family lunch or brunch.

**2. Egiodola** — bistronomy / steak & inventive plates
- **Google 4.9, 113 reviews** (per Heerby restaurant page & TA search result summary: https://heerby.ma/fr/restaurant/4Ab4kJzn7xpT, https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d32931081-Reviews-Egiodola-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html — TA shows 5.0 from 10 reviews).
- Cuisine: French-international bistronomy; signature: perfectly cooked steaks, carpaccio, chef's seasonal plates (https://www.egiodola.ma/).
- Area: Hay Riad (19 Rue Al Ananas). Price: €€€ (TA lists $$$$). Family-friendly: better for a "nice dinner" night than a rowdy kids' meal.
- Lat/long: ~33.9560, -6.8650 (approx.)
- Why go: the highest-rated kitchen in Rabat right now — adult treat dinner.

**3. Restaurant Alaska (ALASKA Bistro)** — Mediterranean bistro/brunch
- **Google 4.8, ~168 reviews** (Heerby 2026: https://heerby.ma/en/blog/best-restaurants-rabat); RG 4.5 from 261 reviews (https://restaurantguru.com/RESTAURANT-ALASKA-Rabat); TA 5.0 (https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d28569113-Reviews-Restaurant_Alaska_Rabat-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html).
- Cuisine: Mediterranean bistro; signature: brunch plates, excellent coffee, terrace lunches. Limited hours (afternoons/early evenings; longer weekend brunch).
- Area: Avenue de France, Agdal. Price: €€. Family-friendly: yes, especially weekend brunch.
- Lat/long: ~33.9890, -6.8610 (approx.)
- Why go: dependable, high-quality casual meal in Agdal with a terrace.

**4. Da Milena** — Italian (wood-fired pizza)
- **RG 4.7** (169–294 reviews depending on date: https://restaurantguru.com/Da-Milena-Rabat); TA 5.0 (https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d28317283-Reviews-Da_Milena-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html). Heerby quotes 4.7 from 294 reviews and calls the lasagna "one of the best in Morocco" (https://heerby.ma/en/blog/best-restaurants-rabat). Google rating not directly verifiable in search — RG figure mirrors Google.
- Cuisine: Italian; signature: wood-fired pizza (thin crispy crust), lasagna, tiramisu.
- Area: Prestigia / Riad Al Andalous, Hay Riad (south Rabat — short taxi). Price: €€. **Family-friendly: one of the best kid options in town.**
- Lat/long: ~33.9420, -6.8870 (approx.)
- Why go: authentic Italian run by caring owners — the easy "kids are tired of tagine" night.

**5. Dar Rbatia** — traditional Moroccan in an 18th-century medina house
- **Google 4.5** (per Mindtrip/aggregator summary: https://mindtrip.ai/restaurant/rabat-sale-kenitra/dar-rbatia/re-CTeIJWlT); TA 4.0 from 417 reviews (https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d1948691-Reviews-Dar_Rbatia-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html).
- Cuisine: classic Rbati cooking, set menus; signature: briouats, lamb tagine, couscous, mint tea ceremony. Open since 1989, restored 18th-century dar.
- Area: deep in the Medina (6 rue Farane Khachane, off Rue Sidi Fateh). Price: €€–€€€ (set menus). Family-friendly: yes — theatrical setting kids enjoy; book ahead.
- Lat/long: ~34.0238, -6.8400 (approx.)
- Why go: the "eating in a Moroccan home" experience without leaving the old town.

### Very good (4.3–4.4 — slightly under the 4.5 bar, flagged)

**6. Dar Zaki** — traditional Moroccan riad-restaurant ⚠ *Google 4.4, just under bar*
- **Google 4.4; RG 4.5 from 1,201 reviews** (https://restaurantguru.com/Dar-Zaki-Rabat); TA page: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d7312105-Reviews-Dar_Zaki-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html.
- Cuisine: Moroccan; signature: chicken pastilla, lamb tagine, vegetable couscous. ~120–250 MAD/head.
- Area: Medina (Rue Sidi Fateh area), Andalusian-style patios + roof terrace. Price: €€. Family-friendly: yes; arrive early or book.
- Lat/long: ~34.0235, -6.8395 (approx.)
- Why go: best-reviewed of the medina's traditional riad restaurants on volume + score combined.

**7. Cosmopolitan Restaurant** — French classics in an Art Deco villa ⚠ *Google 4.4*
- **Google 4.4** (per RG aggregation: https://restaurantguru.com/Cosmopolitan-Rabat); TA 4.3 from 441 reviews (https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d2178770-Reviews-Cosmopolitan_Restaurant-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html).
- Cuisine: refined French; signature: duck, seafood specials, garden-patio dining; excellent wine list (https://www.fodors.com/world/africa-and-middle-east/morocco/the-northern-atlantic-coast/restaurants/reviews/restaurant-cosmopolitan-607959).
- Area: Av. Ibn Toumert, Ville Nouvelle (sources also tag it Agdal-side). Price: €€€. Family-friendly: smart-casual; fine with well-fed kids, better as a parents' choice.
- Lat/long: ~34.0150, -6.8390 (approx.)
- Why go: Rabat's classic French table in a 1930s villa.

**8. Ty Potes** — French/Breton crêperie ⚠ *TA 4.3; Google rating not verifiable in search*
- **TA 4.3, ~#13 of 444** (https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d2521217-Reviews-Ty_Potes-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html); Facebook 94% recommend (https://www.facebook.com/TyPotes/). Could not verify a Google score — flagging per the brief.
- Cuisine: savoury buckwheat galettes + sweet crêpes, salads, cheese boards; signature: Texan savoury crêpe, chestnut crêpe.
- Area: Rue Tobrouk, Hassan district (near Hassan Tower). Price: €€. **Family-friendly: crêpes + a garden = easy win with kids.**
- Lat/long: ~34.0163, -6.8330 (approx.)
- Why go: relaxed garden lunch between Hassan Tower and the centre; kids love it.

**9. Le Petit Beur (Dar Tajine)** — old-school Moroccan with live oud music ⚠ *RG 4.3*
- **RG 4.3 from 1,135 reviews** (https://restaurantguru.com/Le-petit-beur-Rabat); TA ~#8 of 416 with 484 reviews (https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d779960-Reviews-Le_Petit_Beur-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html). Google not directly verified.
- Cuisine: Moroccan since 1994; signature: tagines, couscous royale, pastilla; evening live Andalusian music.
- Area: 8 Rue Damas, Ville Nouvelle (near Rabat Ville station). Price: €€. Family-friendly: yes, early evening.
- Why go: dinner-with-music institution, walkable from most central hotels.
- Lat/long: ~34.0146, -6.8322 (approx.)

### Icons to know about — below the bar, flagged honestly

**10. Dar Naji** — Moroccan, famous acrobatic mint-tea pouring ⚠ *Google 4.2*
- **Google 4.2** (main branch; Agdal branch 4.1 — RG aggregation: https://restaurantguru.com/Dar-Naji-Rabat-2); TA: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d14977137-Reviews-Restaurant_Dar_Naji-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html.
- Cuisine: cheap-and-cheerful traditional Moroccan; signature: tangia, tagines, Friday couscous, theatrical tea service. Generous portions, low prices.
- Area: Bab El Had, on the SW corner of the Medina. Price: €. **Family-friendly: very — buzzing, fast, fun for kids.**
- Lat/long: ~34.0175, -6.8425 (approx., at Bab El Had).
- Why go: the classic first-lunch-in-Morocco spot; go for atmosphere and tea-pouring, accept variable consistency.

**11. Dinarjat** — grand Andalusian-house Moroccan ⚠ *TA 3.9 / RG 4.0 — below bar*
- **RG 4.0 from 1,338 reviews; TA 3.9, #24 of 433** (https://restaurantguru.com/Dinarjat-Rabat, https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d779615-Reviews-Dinarjat-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html).
- Cuisine: high-end Moroccan in a 1908 medina mansion, live folkloric music; signature: lamb tagine with almonds, chicken tagine with preserved lemon, pastilla.
- Area: Medina, near the Oudaias side (unsigned door — ring the bell). Price: €€€. Reviews say "go for the room, not value".
- Lat/long: ~34.0270, -6.8370 (approx.)
- Why go: the most beautiful dining room in Rabat — but ratings say it's an atmosphere buy.

**12. Le Dhow** — restaurant-boat on the Bou Regreg ⚠ *RG 3.8 / TA 3.5 — below bar*
- **RG 3.8 from 5,966 reviews; TA 3.5 from 922 reviews** (https://restaurantguru.com/Le-Dhow-Rabat, https://www.tripadvisor.co.za/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d3607848-Reviews-Le_Dhow-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html).
- A real moored dhow: café deck, restaurant deck, lounge below (https://www.fodors.com/world/africa-and-middle-east/morocco/the-northern-atlantic-coast/things-to-do/nightlife/reviews/le-dhow-607863).
- Area: Rabat bank of the river, below the Hassan side, facing the marina. Price: €€–€€€.
- Lat/long: ~34.0265, -6.8320 (approx.)
- Why go: **drinks/ice-cream stop with a knockout view**, not a destination dinner — reviews consistently say stick to drinks. Kids enjoy being on a boat.

**13. La Mamma** — candle-lit Italian, wood-fired oven ⚠ *RG 4.1 / TA 3.7 — below bar*
- **RG 4.1 from 1,598 reviews; TA 3.7 from 357 reviews** (https://restaurantguru.com/La-Mamma-Rabat, https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293736-d983216-Reviews-La_Mamma-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html). Reviews flag indoor smoking — **prefer Da Milena for the family pizza night.**
- Area: Rue Tanta, behind Hotel Balima, Ville Nouvelle. Price: €€. Lat/long: ~34.0185, -6.8345 (approx.)

### Cafés / patisserie / view stops

**14. Café des Oudayas (Café Maure)** — iconic kasbah tea terrace ⚠ *RG 4.0*

## 3. Sights & experiences

**1. Kasbah des Oudaias** — 12th-c. Almohad citadel over the river mouth; blue-and-white lanes, ocean views.
- Rating: **~4.5 from ~7,900 reviews** (aggregated Google figure per https://citytoursmorocco.com/attractions/kasbah-udayas); TA reviews: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293736-d481071-Reviews-Kasbah_des_Oudaias-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html.
- Lat/long: **34.0317, -6.8359** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasbah_of_the_Udayas).
- Practical: free, always open; 1–2h; politely decline "unofficial guides" at the gate. Semaphore platform at the tip = best river-mouth view. UNESCO-listed.

**2. Andalusian Gardens** — formal garden inside the Kasbah walls (French-era, Andalusian style); cats, storks, bougainvillea.
- Rating: not separately verifiable on Google; consistently praised on TA (https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293736-d2345005-Reviews-Andalusian_Gardens-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html).
- Lat/long: ~34.0306, -6.8348 (approx., within Kasbah).
- Practical: free, sunrise–sunset; 30 min; combine with Café Maure next door. Go early for empty paths.

**3. Hassan Tower + Mausoleum of Mohammed V** — unfinished 12th-c. minaret (44 m) and forest of columns; opposite, the white-marble royal mausoleum with mounted guards.
- Rating: **~4.4 from ~8,200 reviews** (aggregated per https://citytoursmorocco.com/attractions/hassan-tower); TA: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293736-d318090-Reviews-Hassan_Tower-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html.
- Lat/long: **34.0212, -6.8204** (esplanade; https://latitude.to/satellite-map/ma/morocco/25170/hassan-tower) — tower at the north end (~34.0241), mausoleum south end.
- Practical: free; mausoleum open ~08:00–18:30 but avoid ~12:00–14:00 (closes for prayers, esp. Friday: https://www.frommers.com/destinations/rabat/attractions/le-tour-hassan--mausoleum-of-mohammed-v/); ~1h; modest dress; non-Muslims may view the mausoleum interior from the gallery.

**4. Chellah** — walled Merinid necropolis over Roman ruins (Sala Colonia), storks nesting on the minaret, gardens, eel pool legend. Reopened after major restoration.
- Rating: **Google 4.5 from ~5,400 reviews; TA 4.5 from ~1,360** (aggregator figures surfaced in search; TA: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293736-d481066-Reviews-Chellah-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html; official site: https://www.chellah.site/en/practical-information/).
- Lat/long: **34.0067, -6.8203** (https://latitude.to/satellite-map/ma/morocco/22552/chellah).
- Practical: daily 08:30–18:30; ~70 MAD adult (~€6.5), audio-guide option ~120 MAD; 1–1.5h; English signage post-renovation, no guide needed. Late afternoon = golden light + storks.

**5. Medina of Rabat + Rue des Consuls** — relaxed, hassle-light old town (part of the UNESCO listing); Rue des Consuls is the historic craft street (Rbati carpets, leather, babouches).
- Rating: TA attraction page: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293736-d481119-Reviews-Medina_of_Rabat-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html; Rue des Consuls: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293736-d23035646-Reviews-Rue_Des_Consuls-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html. Google rating not separately verifiable.
- Lat/long: medina centroid ~34.0250, -6.8370 (approx.)
- Practical: free; Monday & Thursday mornings the street becomes a carpet market (https://www.fodors.com/world/africa-and-middle-east/morocco/the-northern-atlantic-coast/things-to-do/shopping); prices fairer and haggling gentler than Marrakech; 1–2h.

**6. Mohammed VI Museum of Modern & Contemporary Art (MMVI)** — Morocco's flagship modern-art museum (opened 2014); 200+ Moroccan artists.
- Rating: Google not verified; TA page: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293736-d4043308-Reviews-Museum_Mohamed_VI_of_Modern_and_Contemporary_Art-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html (Trip.com shows 3.6: https://us.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/rabat/mohammed-vi-museum-of-modern-and-contemporary-art-38910519/).
- Lat/long: ~34.0144, -6.8333 (corner Av. Mohammed V / Av. Moulay Hassan, approx.)
- Practical: 10:00–18:00, **closed Tuesday**; modest fee (discounts; free Fri for residents — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_VI_Museum_of_Modern_and_Contemporary_Art); 1–1.5h; air-conditioned = good midday-heat escape.

**7. Villa des Arts** — two art-deco villas + gardens with rotating contemporary exhibitions.
- Rating: **Google 4.4 from ~545 reviews** (https://villa-des-arts.wheree.com/ and https://www.postcard.inc/places/villa-des-arts-rabat-dmazQ63Nccr).
- Lat/long: ~34.0125, -6.8390 (10 Rue Beni Mellal, Hassan, approx.)
- Practical: **free**; Tue–Sun ~09:00/09:30–19:00, closed Mon; 45 min.

**8. Royal Palace (Dar al-Makhzen) & Mechouar** — the King's official residence; visitors see the gates, guards and the vast mechouar esplanade only.
- Rating: Google not verifiable; background: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Palace_of_Rabat, https://www.visitrabat.com/en/lieux/the-royal-palace/.
- Lat/long: **34.0017, -6.8410** (https://mapcarta.com/W1104199416).
- Practical: exterior/esplanade only, carry ID; quick 20–30 min photo stop en route to Chellah.

**9. Plage de Rabat / Plage des Oudayas + Bou Regreg riverfront** — town beach below the Kasbah; beginner surf schools; sunset views to the Kasbah. ⚠ mixed reviews (litter; river-mouth water quality) — treat as a stroll/surf-watch, not a swim day.
- Rating: TA mixed: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293736-d1382967-Reviews-Plage_de_Rabat-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html; surf guide: https://www.story-rabat.com/rabat-beach/.
- Lat/long: ~34.0300, -6.8390 (approx.)
- Practical: free; combine with Kasbah; cleaner swimming beaches are south at Harhoura/Temara (short taxi).

**10. Jardin Zoologique de Rabat (National Zoo)** — modern 2012 zoo, African fauna incl. Atlas lions (descendants of royal lions), giraffe feeding.
- Rating: Google not directly verified; Facebook 88% recommend (~2,016 reviews: https://www.facebook.com/RabatZoo/); TA #4 of 69 things to do, 443 reviews (https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293736-d15553839-Reviews-Jardin_Zoologique_de_Rabat-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html).
- Lat/long: ~33.9540, -6.8830 (Hay Riad/Témara side, approx.)
- Practical: Mar–Oct 10:00–17:30; ~70 MAD adult / 50 MAD child; giraffe feeding +50 MAD, reptile house +20 MAD (https://www.rabatzoo.ma/en/prices/); allow 3h; **the kids' highlight**.

**11. Mohammed VI Tower** — 250 m, 55-floor rocket-shaped skyscraper on the Salé bank; Africa's (joint-)tallest; **opened April 2026** with a panoramic observation deck.
- Rating: too new for meaningful review volume (no verified Google rating; news: https://www.dezeen.com/2026/05/06/mohammed-vi-tower-morocco-tallest-building-rafael-de-la%E2%80%91hoz-hakim-benjelloun/; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_VI_Tower).
- Lat/long: **34.0191, -6.8051** (https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q25452628).
- Practical: check locally for observation-deck tickets/hours — brand-new in 2026; visible from everywhere; great from the Hassan Tower esplanade at dusk.

**12. Salé — medina, Grand Mosque & Madrasa of Abu al-Hasan; Bouregreg Marina** — cross the river by rowboat for the untouristed twin city: 14th-c. Marinid madrasa (rich carving, rooftop view), authentic souks, then the marina promenade.
- Rating: madrasa well-reviewed on TA (https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g660175-d20425846-Reviews-Madrasa_of_Abu_al_Hasan-Sale_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html); marina: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g660175-d15138981-Reviews-Bouregreg_Marina-Sale_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html.
- Lat/long: Salé medina centroid ~34.0380, -6.8240 (approx.); marina ~34.0290, -6.8230 (approx.)
- Practical: rowboat crossing 2.5–5 MAD/person, a few minutes (https://www.arabnews.com/node/1742556/middle-east); madrasa entry ~10 MAD; expect some hustlers near the madrasa; marina promenade is stroller-flat, family-friendly, lively in the evening.

## 4. Suggested 3-night itinerary

**Day 1 — Friday 19 June (land 11:20):**

## 5. Neighbourhood guide

- **Kasbah des Oudaias** — the postcard: whitewashed-and-blue lanes inside 12th-c. walls on the headland. No hotels of size, a handful of tiny guesthouses; zero traffic; everything photogenic. Stay adjacent (medina) rather than inside. Walk: 0 min to gardens/Café Maure, 5 min to beach, 10 min to Rue des Consuls.
- **Medina** — flat, compact, far calmer than Fès/Marrakech; souks, riads, Rue des Consuls crafts. **Best base for sightseeing on foot** (Kasbah 10 min, Hassan Tower 20 min, tram + station 10–15 min). Evenings are lively but family-safe on main lanes.
- **Hassan** — the monument-and-ministries quarter around Hassan Tower; wide boulevards, Villa des Arts, Ty Potes. Quiet at night. Good mid-point base; walkable to both river and centre.
- **Ville Nouvelle / Centre (Ave Mohammed V)** — French-era boulevard with the parliament, cathedral, Rabat Ville station, cafés and Pâtisserie Majestic. Practical, central, less charming; most chain/business hotels and Le Petit Beur, La Mamma here.
- **Agdal** — leafy modern district SW of centre: brunch spots (Alaska), Amoud patisserie, shopping streets. Local life, not sights; 10-min taxi/tram to the centre. Good restaurants justify one dinner trip even if you stay elsewhere.
- **Souissi** — embassy/villa district further out: Villa Mandarine, Sofitel, big gardens. Tranquil and green; you'll taxi everywhere (10–15 min to sights).
- **Salé / Bouregreg Marina side** — across the river: the marina strip (Fairmont, Le Dhow opposite, seafood restaurants, flat promenade) is modern and family-easy; the Salé medina behind it is the authentic, untouristed counterpoint. Rowboat or tram link to Rabat in minutes. Stay here for resort comfort + views back at the Kasbah.

## 6. Practical facts

- **June weather:** avg high **25.4°C**, avg low **18.7°C**, ~2.8 rain days (3 mm) all month, ~69% humidity, longest daylight of the year (~14h24m); **sea ~21.8°C** — bracing but swimmable (https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/morocco/rabat-weather-june, https://www.seatemperature.org/africa/morocco/rabat-june.htm). Atlantic breeze keeps evenings mild — pack a light layer.
- **Currency:** Moroccan dirham (MAD), closed currency — exchange/withdraw on arrival. **1 EUR ≈ 10.69 MAD** (11 Jun 2026: https://www.xe.com/currencyconverter/convert/?Amount=1&From=EUR&To=MAD). Cards OK in hotels/nicer restaurants; carry cash for souks, taxis, rowboats, tips.
- **Airport → city:** RBA is ~10–15 km NE of the centre. Official taxis outside arrivals, 24/7: **~150 MAD day / 200 MAD night fixed to central Rabat, 15–25 min** (https://www.welcomepickups.com/rabat/airport-to-city/, https://aeroport-rabat.com/transport/taxi/). No direct tram/train from the airport.
- **Trams:** 2 lines (L1 crosses the Hassan II bridge Rabat↔Salé), single ticket **7 MAD** (raised from 6 MAD, valid 60 min with transfers: https://www.tram-way.ma/en/ticket-and-fines/, https://barlamantoday.com/2025/06/26/rabat-sale-tramway-to-increase-ticket-subscription-prices-starting-july-1/); runs ~06:00–22:30 (Sun from ~07:00) every ~8 min peak (https://www.tramguide.com/trams-in-rabat-sale-schedule-lines-fares-tickets-map/). No stop right at the Kasbah — nearest is Bab Chellah/medina side.
- **Petit taxis:** blue in Rabat, metered, max 3 passengers (a family of 4 may need to charm or take two/Careem app); cross-city rides 10–30 MAD.
- **Friday:** the mosque day — Friday couscous is the national lunch ritual; some small shops/medina stalls close ~12:00–15:00; the Mohammed V Mausoleum closes to visitors around midday prayers; everything else functions normally (https://bluedoorcuisine.com/8357/, https://www.frommers.com/destinations/rabat/attractions/le-tour-hassan--mausoleum-of-mohammed-v/).
- **Dress code:** Rabat is a relaxed capital — normal summer clothes fine. At the **Mausoleum of Mohammed V** (a royal tomb) cover shoulders and knees; same respect-level at mosques/madrasas and helpful in the Kasbah/medina (https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293736-d477266-Reviews-Mausoleum_of_Mohammad_V-Rabat_Rabat_Sale_Kenitra.html). Swimwear only on the beach.
- **Tipping:** cafés 5–10%; restaurants 10–15% in cash to the server; taxis round up 2–5 MAD; porters 5–10 MAD/bag; riad staff ~100–150 MAD for a short stay; guides 100–200 MAD/day (https://tasteofmaroc.com/tipping-in-morocco/, https://morocco-tips.com/tipping-in-morocco/).
- **Trains/day trips:** Casablanca ~1h (Al Boraq hourly from Rabat Agdal, 2nd class 89–172 MAD; classic trains from central Rabat Ville) (https://alboraqtrain.com/rabat-to-casablanca-by-al-boraq-schedules-prices-how-to-book/, https://www.seat61.com/train-travel-in-morocco.htm). **Fès ~2.5–3h each way — too far for this 3-night trip**; likewise Marrakech. Book at https://www.oncf-voyages.ma/.
- **Misc:** tap water generally treated but bottled recommended for visitors; Maghreb time = Irish time +0/+1 depending on DST quirks (Morocco stays on GMT+1 year-round outside Ramadan — in June 2026 Morocco is **same time as Dublin**); plugs European 2-pin (bring adapters from Ireland's G).

### Coastline & river description (for the stylised map)

- The Atlantic shore at Rabat runs **roughly SW→NE** (the ocean lies to the **NW**). Drawn on a page with north up, the coast is a diagonal from lower-left (Harhoura/Temara beaches) to upper-right (Salé's beaches).
- The **Bou Regreg river** flows in from the **SE interior** and bends north, meeting the sea between two long breakwaters at ~34.033, -6.834. It is the historic frontier: **Rabat occupies the LEFT (south/west) bank; Salé the RIGHT (north/east) bank.**
- The **Kasbah des Oudaias** sits on a rocky headland on the Rabat side at the very river mouth — the visual "full stop" where city wall meets ocean. The **town beach (Plage des Oudayas)** tucks just outside/west of the Kasbah on the open ocean; Salé's wider beach mirrors it across the jetties.
- Going **upstream (SE) from the mouth, Rabat bank:** Kasbah → medina river edge (Borj ed-Dar bastion) → Le Dhow moored boat → Bab Lamrissa/Hassan slope with the **Hassan Tower + Mausoleum** on the bluff ~1.5 km from the mouth → Rabat Ville station ~1 km inland → **Chellah** ~3 km upstream on a hill above the river's flood plain.
- **Salé bank, same direction:** Salé beach → Salé medina walls (Grand Mosque + madrasa inside) → **Bouregreg Marina** (the pleasure-boat basin, with the Fairmont) roughly opposite Le Dhow → **Mohammed VI Tower**, the unmissable 250 m rocket ~2.5 km upstream, just N of the river.
- Two crossings matter for the map: the **Hassan II bridge** (road + tram L1) mid-way, and the **blue rowboats** shuttling between the medina quay and the marina near the mouth.
- Inland on the Rabat side, the city steps SW away from the river: medina (oceanfront) → Ville Nouvelle/Av. Mohammed V (parallel to the coast) → Royal Palace compound → Agdal → Souissi → zoo/Hay Riad in the far SW. The **airport is NE**, inland on the Salé side of the river, ~10 km from the centre.
