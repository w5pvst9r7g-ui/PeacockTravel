Know the ground

Rabat in five quarters

Locked in · day one

Friday is Casablanca day

Straight off the plane and onto the rails: bags to the riad, then the ~13:30 train from Rabat Ville to Casa Voyageurs (about an hour). The 16:00 Friday guided tour of the Hassan II Mosque (4.8★, ~48k reviews) is the centrepiece — one of the only mosques in Morocco non-Muslims can enter, its minaret rising 210 m over the Atlantic. Stroll the Corniche, take an early dinner at Rick's Café (doors 18:30 — book), and the train has everyone home in Rabat by bedtime.

  • 🚆 Rabat Ville ⇄ Casa Voyageurs · ~1h · every 30–60 min
  • 🕌 Friday tours 09:00 / 10:00 / 15:00 / 16:00 · 140 MAD adults, 30 MAD kids 6+
  • 👟 Comfortable shoes; shoulders & knees covered
